# Decision: Position as a niche applied-research bridge-builder, funded by Barista-FIRE, executed as an AI director+verifier

## Decision

Adopt a long-term (5–10 year) career-positioning strategy for the repo owner as an AI/ML engineer:

1. **Objective = freedom + meaning + "enough"**, not max wealth or control. Drop the founder/equity
   path (owner-ranked last).
2. **Barista-FIRE at 45 is the engine, not a rival, for the research goal.** US-salary/Poland-cost
   makes ~2.0M PLN by 45 realistic (~6.5k/month passive + wife's income + light work). Protect the
   savings rate; avoid lifestyle creep.
3. **Redefine "recognized researcher"** from frontier inventor (unrealistic given newborn + solo +
   FIRE + not-all-in) to **niche bridge-builder between RDL / tabular FMs and real relational
   field-sales data** — exploiting the production-data asset academics lack.
4. **Execution levers:** sequence the job *trust → infra → ambitious models*; build reputation as a
   sustainable ~1-artifact-per-quarter side-stream; become an **AI director + verifier** (context
   engineering, plan→execute→verify, parallel/background agents) so a solo ML+DE does team-scale work;
   negotiate **publishing rights + salary, not equity**; treat **eval-driven development** as the
   triple-duty skill.

Full plan: [knowledge/career/plan.md](../knowledge/career/plan.md). Day-one playbook:

## Context

Owner is starting at SPOTIO (US field-sales SaaS, remote from Gdańsk) owning all ML features, solo,
salary-only, with a 3-month-old and a Barista-FIRE-at-45 goal. Genuine research interest in relational
deep learning + tabular foundation models — which happens to be the *best-fit* architecture for the
field-sales next-best-action problem (this NBA repo is the applied prototype). The conversation asked
how to position over the next 5–10 years toward possible AGI, then how to prepare via AI tooling
(Cursor/agents) for tasks a solo engineer must cover.

## Alternatives considered

- **Maximize income / go founder for ownership/equity.** Rejected: conflicts with owner's stated
  priorities (founder ranked last) and the parenting/not-all-in constraint.
- **Chase frontier ML research.** Rejected: needs full-time obsession / a lab; incompatible with
  newborn + solo + FIRE; would damage the higher-priority freedom/meaning goals.
- **Be the best possible "worker"/fast coder in the layer being automated.** Rejected: that layer is
  exactly what AI commoditizes; durable value is in specification, verification, judgment, and owned
  assets (here: FIRE portfolio + niche reputation).
- **Lead with ambitious models (RDL/tabular FM) at the new job.** Rejected: over-engineering before
  trust/infra is the top way to lose ML-ownership credibility; sequence trust → infra → ambition.

## Reasoning

The owner's constraints and rankings, taken seriously, collapse into one coherent strategy: let
financial resilience (nearly a default given the salary/cost arbitrage) fund a sustainable,
parenting-compatible push toward a *reachable* niche research reputation, while AI tooling provides the
leverage for a solo engineer to deliver and buy back time. The same path yields financial freedom +
niche recognition + lead-level credibility, so no early forced choice — maximal optionality. It is also
robust across AGI scenarios: financial resilience + judgment/trust + meaning-outside-work are the hedges
that pay off whether AI plateaus or accelerates.

## Trade-offs accepted

- Gives up the upside of the founder/equity path and frontier-research prestige.
- Year one is unglamorous (infra + simple baseline) before the interesting RDL/tabular-FM work.
- Reputation builds slowly (quarterly cadence) to protect parenting time and avoid burnout.
