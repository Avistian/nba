# Career Value in the Agent Era — Rules

Apply by default when advising on AI/ML career positioning or designing agentic workflows that
affect how humans create value.

1. **Optimize for ceiling work, not floor defense.** Do not try to out-type agents at boilerplate;
   shift identity toward judgment, proof, architecture, and accountability.

2. **Treat “AI fluency” as table stakes, not a moat.** Everyone gets the same tools; durable edge is
   domain judgment + eval rigor + system ownership.

3. **Prefer the ML barbell over the middle.** Deepen objective-bound proprietary ML _and/or_ bring
   ML rigor to AI-native systems; avoid undifferentiated “standard train-and-ship.”

4. **Make proof the product.** Specs, tests, adversarial evals, promotion gates, and release
   records are the career-visible artifacts — not demos alone.

5. **Own understanding even when you outsource thinking.** You can delegate implementation; you
   cannot safely delegate mental models of what shipped and why.

6. **Design human checkpoints as architecture, not failure.** Escalation on product/security/
   ambiguous decisions is a feature of trustworthy agent systems.

7. **Compound with an agentic experimentation loop.** Keep human taste on hypothesis selection and
   result interpretation; let agents run mechanical sweeps and tabulation.

8. **Invest growth time in artifacts, not content.** Default split: ship loop > evals > deep edge >
   agentic drills > reading. Courses without a deployed/eval artifact within 30 days are a fail.
